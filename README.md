An offline browser web app built for the [make.fun gta6-cover-art-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

Turn your photos and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art: the iconic multi-panel
poster and loading-screen look with a Vice City neon-sunset grade. Upload one or
more photos, arrange them across the panels with crop and position per panel,
letter your caption in bold original type, and export a share-ready PNG in
1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080 — all offline, your photos never leave your machine.

## How it works

- Upload photos (multiple) or start from the built-in demo panels
- Multi-panel cover composition with per-panel crop and position
- Vice City sunset / neon grade applied to every panel
- Bold original lettering with an editable caption — no Rockstar logos, art or fonts
- One-click PNG export: 1080x1080, 1080x1350, 1920x1080

Open `app/index.html` in a browser — no build step, no network needed.
